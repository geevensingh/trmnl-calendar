# TRMNL Calendar

A filtered calendar display for a TRMNL device.

## Language

**Agenda**:
A chronological list of calendar events selected for display.
_Avoid_: Event list

**Exclusion rule**:
A configurable condition that identifies calendar events to omit from the agenda.
_Avoid_: Hide rule

**Working day**:
A Monday-through-Friday calendar date, whether or not it is a public holiday.
_Avoid_: Business day

**Primary day**:
The first working day on or after the current date in the agenda's timezone.
_Avoid_: Today (when referring to the first displayed day)

**Secondary day**:
The working day immediately after the primary day.
_Avoid_: Tomorrow (when referring to the second displayed day)
